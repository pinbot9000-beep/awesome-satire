# Contributing

Thank you for considering a contribution to Awesome Satire.

## Pull Request Guidelines

- **One addition per pull request.** Multiple entries in a single PR will be asked to be split.
- **Search the README before submitting** to avoid duplicate entries.
- **Place the entry in the appropriate section** — publications, newsletters, podcasts, Telegram channels, etc.
- **Within sections, maintain alphabetical order** where alphabetical order is the convention. For the country-grouped publications section, group first by country, then alphabetically.
- **Title your PR clearly**: "Add [Name] to [Section]"

## Entry Format

```
- [Name](url) — One-line description ending with a period.
```

- Use an em dash (—) between the name and the description, not a hyphen.
- Begin descriptions with a capital letter; end with a period.
- Keep descriptions to one sentence focused on what makes the entry distinctive.
- For Telegram channels, use the `https://t.me/channelname` format.
- For X / Twitter accounts, link to `https://x.com/handle`.
- For subreddits, link to `https://reddit.com/r/subname`.

## Quality Standards

To be added, an entry must be:

1. **Explicitly satirical.** Not generally humorous, not adjacent comedy. The publication, channel, or account must self-identify or be widely recognized as satirical, parodic, or comedic in the news-and-commentary tradition.
2. **Active.** Publishing or posting within the last 6 months. Defunct entries belong in the Historical & Classic section only if they had a meaningful cultural footprint.
3. **Reachable.** Working URL. Suspended accounts, dead links, and paywalled-only sites without any public-facing content will be rejected.
4. **Distinctive.** The one-line description should explain what makes the entry worth listing — its angle, its country, its tone, its specialty.

## What We Don't Accept

- Personal humor blogs without a satirical news or commentary focus.
- Conspiracy or hoax sites that present false information as factual.
- Sites that no longer publish (with the Historical & Classic exception above).
- Affiliate links, sponsored placements, or advertising URLs.
- Self-promotion of a single individual artist, comedian, or anonymous account unless they have a recognized body of satirical work.
- Entries that exist solely to attack a single person, group, or viewpoint without broader satirical framing.

## Political Balance

We accept submissions across the political spectrum. Entries are evaluated on whether they meet the criteria above, not on the political stance of their satire. The Onion, The Babylon Bee, Le Gorafi, and Der Postillon all coexist in this directory because they are all recognized satirical publications with active output.

If you believe an existing entry doesn't meet the criteria, please open an issue rather than a removal PR.
